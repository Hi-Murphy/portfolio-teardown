Implementation
--------------
`apps/web/next.config.ts` reads `process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_EAGER_3D` once and exposes it as the bare
compile-time constant `__XP_EAGER_3D__`. `apps/web/eager-3d.d.ts` declares the constant as boolean.
The hero and workbench use it only around their passive interaction wait; the click path, reduced
motion and saveData handling are unchanged.

The string-valued `compiler.define` form recommended in the revision was tried first
(`'true'` / `'false'`), but Next 16.4.3 JSON-serializes define values, making `'false'` a truthy
string at runtime. A plain boolean define is therefore used; Turbopack 16.4.3 honours it
(`build/turbopack-build/impl.js` passes `createDefineEnv` into `createProject`). The flag-off build
folds to the exact T014 route chunks.
