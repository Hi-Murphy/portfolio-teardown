Date: 2026-11-13

Target: static export at http://localhost:PORT/
Browser: Playwright Chromium (headless)
Theme/motion: light, normal motion for the storyboard; 390 dark capture

## Sequence implemented

implode → explode → zoomIn ×3 → capture-path follow → inspect-path follow → zoomReset +
scrolled-stage reset → two quarter turns → implode → hold → repeat.

The pure controller owns phase transitions, injected actions, the scheduler, and the follow driver.
All delays derive from xploder/motion tokens. The camera follower writes only stage.scrollTop,
interpolating in animation frames. The only engine DOM reads are the documented stage and
data-xploder-trace-pad contract.
